I'm a software engineer from Chennai.

I'm not the most talented person in the room. But I'm probably the most obsessed. When something catches my attention, I don't let go — I go deep, stay late, and keep going until I actually understand it.

I put in the hours quietly. And when someone around me is stuck, I show up — that part has never felt optional to me.

I don't ask people for help easily. When I hit a wall, I figure it out — documentation, AI tools, whatever it takes. I'd rather spend three hours solving something myself than interrupt someone for a five-minute answer. That's just how I'm wired.

I'm drawn to how things work beneath the surface — systems, infrastructure, the kind of engineering nobody sees but everything depends on.

Space is the one thing that never gets old for me. The scale of it — a small planet circling an ordinary star in one of two trillion galaxies — and yet here we are, writing code. I think about that more than I probably should.

I also read about history and philosophy. Civilizations rise, collapse, and get forgotten. It's a useful thing to sit with.
